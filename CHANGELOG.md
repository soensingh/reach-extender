## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-26

The first public release, for macOS 14 Sonoma and later.

### Added

- **Animated pointer** that replaces the macOS cursor, drawn on a see-through, click-through layer above every app, so clicks land exactly where they always do.
- **Four pointer styles,** all cartoon hands: the glove, a point with the thumb out, a fist and a middle finger.
- **Right or left hand:** the right hand reaches in from the bottom right (the default); the left hand mirrors it from the bottom left.
- **Six preset colors** (white, yellow, coral, blue, green, purple), plus any custom color.
- **Size slider**, from small to huge.
- **The arm:** a long stick that reaches up from below the screen and swings as you move. You can turn it off.
- **Tap animation** on every click: the pointer winds up, then strikes.
- **Tap flashes** that build with quick clicks: sparkle, burst, big burst, star, splat.
- **Flash color:** Automatic (picks black or white from the colors under the click; needs Screen Recording access), Black or White.
- **Mood slider**, from *Shy finger* to *In your face*: how far the glove winds up and how soon the flashes start.
- **Tap sounds:** Tap, Knock, Tick, Pop or your own sound file, with a volume control.
- **Presentation Mode:** clicks only make the pointer tap and don't reach apps. Needs Accessibility access.
- **Optional real cursor** for the text I-beam, resize arrows and crosshairs.
- **Multiple displays:** the pointer and its arm follow you across screens.
- **Keyboard shortcuts:** ⌃⌥P shows or hides the pointer, ⌃⌥T toggles Presentation Mode. Both can be changed in Settings.
- **Menu bar app** with no Dock icon, a settings window, a welcome window on first launch and an option to open at login.
- **Reset to Defaults** in Settings.
- **Licensing:** a 10-minute free trial, counted only while the pointer is on and you're using your Mac. It warns you at 2 minutes and 30 seconds left, and if it runs out during Presentation Mode the pointer stays until you leave it.
  - A one-time lifetime license for one Mac. After you pay, the app unlocks by itself.
  - Your key is emailed to you and can be restored by email.
  - **Move License to This Mac** for when your old Mac is gone.
  - Contact Support from inside the app.
- **Automatic updates** (Sparkle): a daily check and an "Update available" note in the menu. Updates are never installed in the middle of your presentation.
