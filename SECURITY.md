# Security policy

## Supported versions

Security fixes go into the latest release. Please update before reporting.

| Version | Supported |
|---|---|
| 0.1.x (latest) | Yes |
| Anything older | No |

Reach Extender runs on macOS 14 Sonoma and later.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please don't report security problems in public issues or discussions.**

Report them privately, either:

- by email to **soensingh@gmail.com** with "Security" in the subject, or
- through GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button on this repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/soensingh/reach-extender/security), if it's shown.

Helpful details:

- the app version (Finder → Applications → Reach Extender → Get Info) and the macOS version;
- what you found and what an attacker could do with it;
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept;
- whether you've told anyone else.

## What happens next

- You'll get a reply within a few days confirming the report was received.
- We'll keep you posted while we look into it and work on a fix.
- Once a fix ships, we're happy to credit you in the release notes, if you'd like.

Please give us a reasonable amount of time to fix the issue before you disclose it publicly.

## Scope

In scope: the Reach Extender app, its installer, and the website at https://reachextender.soensingh.com, including purchase and license-key handling.

Out of scope: spam, social engineering, denial-of-service attacks and problems in third-party services we don't control.

There's no paid bug bounty at the moment.
